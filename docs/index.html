⌘ ⇧ X

From click to query in seconds

No more guessing at identifiers or walking the hierarchy by hand.

Pick

Press ⌘⇧X and choose a window, like the ⌘⇧5 picker. Connected devices appear as buttons on the edge of the screen.

Inspect

See the window's screenshot beside its full element hierarchy. Click an element in either one to select it.

Copy

Get two queries for the element, by identifier or label and by hierarchy position. Click one to copy it.

// by identifier / label
app.buttons["Add to Cart"]

// by hierarchy position
app.tables.cells.element(boundBy: 2).buttons.firstMatch

Now on real devices

Version 0.4.0 inspects apps on a connected iPhone or iPad.

Pick a device from the edge of your screen

Xamine builds a small XCUITest agent, installs it and connects over the CoreDevice tunnel. What it reads is the same snapshot XCUITest uses, so the queries match exactly.

  • Plug in or unplug and the device list updates right away
  • The agent recovers when the device locks or reconnects
  • Portrait and landscape both line up
  • Follow the foreground app, or choose one

Stop guessing at selectors

Requires macOS with Accessibility and Screen Recording permission. Device inspection needs Xcode and a trusted device with Developer Mode on.
